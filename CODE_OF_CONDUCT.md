# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, appearance, disability, nationality, religion, or viewpoint. Participation
requires respectful, professional behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence constructively.
- Assume good intent while still examining technical claims carefully.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Make space for people with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disclosure of sensitive data, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events,
and other project communication. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior violates this
policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to **security@godel-labs.ai** with the subject
`Tally conduct report`. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Maintainers will limit
details to the people needed to review the report and will avoid conflicts of interest where
practical.

Good-faith reports and appeals will be reviewed without retaliation.
